01How to get your Timeline file

  1. Android (recommended) — Phone Settings → Location → Location Services → Timeline → Export Timeline data. Drop the saved Timeline.json here.
  2. iPhone — Google Maps app → profile photo → Settings → Personal content → Export Timeline data.
  3. Google Takeout (older records) — "Location History (Timeline)" at takeout.google.com. Use Records.json or the monthly Semantic Location History JSONs — not Settings.json.

⚠️ Since 2024 Google stores your Timeline on your phone only. That's why "sign in with Google to import" is impossible by design (there is no official API) — and also why this page can process your file with no server at all.

02Privacy — guaranteed by architecture

📵 No uploadThis page has no server that could receive location data. Parsing, rendering, and video encoding all happen in your browser.
🗺️ Only network: map tilesThe base map comes from CARTO. Tile numbers for the visible area are requested — nothing more.
🗑️ No tracesClose the tab and it's gone. No cookies, no storage of your location data.
🔍 VerifiableOne plain, unminified JS file. Check it yourself in DevTools.
