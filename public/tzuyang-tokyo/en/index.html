All 14 Restaurants

Every restaurant from the Tokyo episodes of the Korean variety show “Tzuyang, How Many Meals?” (ENA / Netflix), with real names, Japanese addresses, signature dishes and coordinates — plotted on a map. The show blurred the signboards, but ENA later published the names and addresses itself in three official “Tokyo restaurant list” posts. This page reproduces those lists and adds verified coordinates.

03 Records from the show

127 plates of sushi — at Sushiro Gotanda, plus udon and pudding
A ¥90,000 yakiniku meal — 150g of chateaubriand alone ran about ¥14,000
A 3kg pork steak — at Mallory Pork Steak. Note the largest size on the current public menu is 2kg (“Olympus”), so this was likely a special order
Even level 0.5 is brutal — Yagura-tei's “spiciest miso ramen in the universe” kneads habanero into the noodles themselves
The single most expensive meal — Meat Yazawa Gotanda, a place Choo Sung-hoon has been going to for over 15 years
A Kodoku no Gurume location — Shanway appeared in season 4, episode 9 (moved from Jingumae to Kita-Sando in 2021)

04 Before you go

Expect longer queues right after the broadcast. Places like Fukinuki, Toukaitei and Torihisa are reservation-based or course-only — check before you go. Hours, closing days and prices change; verify on each restaurant's own page.
  • Coordinates — 4 are exact storefront POIs from OpenStreetMap; the other 10 come from the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI) address lookup, accurate to the building block.
  • Two identical coordinates — Joël Robuchon and Nikugatou are both inside Roppongi Hills (6-10-1 Roppongi), on different floors.
  • Address correction — ENA wrote “Shinjuku Flower Building” for Shinchan; the actual building is Shinjuku Flower High Home, 1F #107.
  • Episode mapping — the three lists were posted in the order: Choo Sung-hoon leg, Kim Jaejoong leg, final day. Mapping them to broadcast episode numbers is inferred from posting order and press releases.
